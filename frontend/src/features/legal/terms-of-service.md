## 5. Video resumes

A video resume is a short video (MP4, at most 30 seconds and 50 MB) in which you present yourself to employers. You can have up to three videos and one of them is posted at a time.

A video must not contain:

- nudity or sexual content;
- violence, threats or cruelty;
- hate speech or harassment;
- personal data of other people, or other people filmed without their permission;
- anything illegal.

It must show you and be about your work or skills. Do not upload videos of someone else.

You can unpost or delete your videos at any time. Your posted video can be seen by other signed-in users and by the employers of jobs you apply to, as the Privacy Policy explains.

## 6. Automatic moderation

Every video is checked by an automatic system before it can be posted. The check uses OpenAI, which receives images and the audio of your video, as the Privacy Policy explains. A video that passes is approved and posted. A video that does not pass is rejected and is not shown to others.

The check is automatic and can be wrong. If your video is rejected, you can upload another one or write to us. We do not promise to change a decision.

We do not check chat messages or game messages automatically, but we can act on what users report to us. We may remove content and suspend or close an account that breaks these terms (see section 10).
